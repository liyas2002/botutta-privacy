Botutta Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. About Botutta

Botutta is an automated live-chat bot used to support a YouTube livestream and provide interactive entertainment and community engagement.

2. Information We Access

Botutta may access information available through YouTube API Services, including:

  • Public YouTube channel and livestream information
  • Public live-chat messages
  • Public YouTube channel or display names associated with chat participants
  • Information required to provide chat commands, games, viewer participation, and automated responses

Botutta does not request or intentionally collect users' passwords, payment information, or private account credentials.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through YouTube API Services may be used to:

  • Monitor and respond to relevant livestream chat messages
  • Provide interactive chat features and games
  • Track game-related points, Gems, XP, ranks, and participation
  • Improve the livestream community experience
  • Maintain the operation and reliability of Botutta

4. Data Storage

Some information may be temporarily or persistently stored when necessary for Botutta's features, such as game progress, points, XP, ranks, viewer participation, and technical information required for the service to operate.

We do not sell or rent users' personal information to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Botutta uses YouTube API Services to interact with YouTube livestream and live-chat functionality.

Botutta may also use third-party technology services for automated response generation and service operation. Information shared with such services is limited to what is necessary for the relevant functionality.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Botutta, maintain features, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their participation where applicable.

To request deletion or ask questions about information handled by Botutta, contact the Botutta service owner through the contact information associated with the service.

8. Children's Privacy

Botutta is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Botutta. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or deletion requests, please contact the Botutta service owner through the contact information associated with the service.

12. YouTube API Services

Botutta's use of information received from YouTube API Services complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.
